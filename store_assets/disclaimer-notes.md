# App Disclaimer - Used Car Check Pro

This document contains the complete app disclaimer for internal reference and legal use. This disclaimer appears in the app's store listing, within the app itself, and in all exported reports.

---

## Full Disclaimer Text

Used Car Check Pro is an observation and organization tool. It is designed to help users record and organize their observations when inspecting a used vehicle for potential purchase.

This app:

- Does not replace a professional mechanical inspection
- Does not replace a vehicle history report (such as CARFAX or AutoCheck)
- Does not replace a title check
- Does not replace a recall check
- Does not replace an independent safety evaluation
- Does not certify any vehicle as safe or roadworthy
- Does not confirm the absence of hidden defects
- Does not determine the market value of any vehicle
- Does not recommend whether to purchase any vehicle
- Does not calculate safety scores, reliability scores, or deal scores

Always consult a qualified mechanic and review all relevant official records before making a vehicle purchase decision.

---

## Additional Limitation Notes for Hybrid and Electric Vehicles

The checklist includes the following advisory items for hybrid and electric vehicles:

**Hybrid Battery Advisory (item hyb_note):**
This app cannot determine hybrid battery health, remaining capacity, or high-voltage system condition. A qualified hybrid technician inspection is strongly recommended before purchase.

**Electric Vehicle Battery Advisory (item ev_note):**
This app cannot determine electric vehicle battery state of health, degradation, or accurate remaining range. A qualified EV technician inspection is strongly recommended before purchase.

These advisory items appear as explicit checklist entries so users cannot miss them during the inspection workflow.

3. Protect Old Alex Hub from liability arising from purchase decisions made based on app findings

## Notes for Legal Review

The app is classified as a utility and organizational tool. It does not provide professional advice, mechanical diagnoses, safety certifications, or financial recommendations. All inspection findings entered by the user are the user's own observations. The app facilitates recording and organizing those observations but does not interpret them or render judgments about vehicle condition beyond what the user inputs.

The decision status options available to the user (Still Reviewing, Ask a Mechanic, Request More Info, Considering, Pass, Purchased) are user-selected labels for organizing personal decision-making. They are not recommendations by the developer.
