Your complete inspection stays on your device. After clear onboarding disclosure, Old Alex Hub collects a limited pseudonymous dataset of vehicle attributes, value ranges, checklist statuses, decisions, and allowlisted product events to analyze and improve the app and develop future open-source features. VINs, seller names, listing URLs, notes, precise location, and photos are excluded from that dataset. VIN lookup sends a VIN to Proveh Server and NHTSA. Google AdMob separately processes advertising data. Old Alex Hub does not sell the product dataset or share it for third-party marketing. Settings > Clear All Data deletes local data and requests deletion of records tied to the anonymous installation.
