# Feature Graphic Notes - Used Car Check Pro

## Specifications

- Dimensions: 1024 x 500 pixels
- Format: PNG or JPEG
- File size: Under 1 MB recommended
- No rounded corners required (Google Play crops them automatically)

---

## Design Direction

### Background

- Color: Dark charcoal (#1A1D21)
- Fill the entire canvas with this solid color
- Optionally add a very subtle radial gradient: slightly lighter center (#22262C) fading to the dark edge (#1A1D21)

### App Logo

- Position: Center of the canvas, slightly above vertical center
- The app icon or logo mark should be rendered cleanly at a size appropriate for the canvas (approximately 120 to 140px height)
- Do not use the icon as a tiny element - it should be prominent

### App Name Text

- Text: "Used Car Check Pro"
- Font: Bold, clean sans-serif (match the app's UI font or use Inter Bold, Roboto Bold, or similar)
- Color: White (#FFFFFF)
- Size: Large, readable at thumbnail size - approximately 64 to 72px
- Position: Centered horizontally, below the logo mark

### Tagline Text

- Text: "Inspect smarter before you buy."
- Font: Regular or medium weight, same sans-serif family
- Color: Steel blue (#3B82F6) or light gray (#A0A8B4)
- Size: Approximately 28 to 32px
- Position: Centered horizontally, directly below the app name, with comfortable spacing

### Optional Accent Graphic

- A simplified car silhouette outline in steel blue (#3B82F6) at low opacity (15 to 20%) can be placed in the background as a subtle watermark-style graphic
- Alternatively, a simplified checklist icon (three lines with checkmarks) in steel blue can accent the left or right side of the canvas
- Keep any graphic element subtle so it does not compete with the text

---

## What to Avoid

- Do not include screenshots of the app in the feature graphic (this is a separate Play Store element)
- Do not use busy or photo-realistic backgrounds that reduce legibility
- Do not use text smaller than 24px - it will be unreadable at thumbnail scale
- Do not add borders or frames around the entire graphic
- Do not use gradients that make the text hard to read
- Do not use red or yellow as dominant colors - they conflict with the app's dark, professional aesthetic

---

## Color Reference

| Element | Color | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Background | Dark charcoal | #1A1D21 |
| App name text | White | #FFFFFF |
| Tagline text | Steel blue or light gray | #3B82F6 or #A0A8B4 |
| Accent graphic | Steel blue | #3B82F6 |
| Accent graphic opacity | 15 to 20% | - |

---

## Notes for Designer

The feature graphic appears at the top of the app's Play Store listing on phones and at the top of the listing on tablets and web. It is also used as a banner in some promotional placements. It must look polished at both full size and when displayed as a smaller card or banner thumbnail. Prioritize legibility of the app name over decorative elements.
