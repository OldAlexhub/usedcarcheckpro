## Full Description

Buying a used car is one of the largest purchases most people make, and it is easy to overlook important details during an in-person inspection. Used Car Check Pro gives you a structured, professional-grade checklist to work through at your own pace, so you never leave the lot wondering what you forgot to check.

GUIDED INSPECTION CHECKLIST

The app walks you through a comprehensive, section-by-section checklist covering every major area of a used vehicle. Each item includes a plain-language description explaining what to look for and why it matters. You do not need to be a mechanic to use it - just follow the prompts and record what you observe.

INSPECTION SECTIONS COVERED

- Documents and Seller: Title, ownership, service records, history reports, lien status, odometer, and sale terms
- Exterior and Body: Paint consistency, panel gaps, rust, glass, all lights, mirrors, bumpers, and seals
- Tires and Wheels: Tread depth on all four tires, wear patterns, sidewall condition, spare tire, and wheel condition
- Interior and Controls: Seats, seatbelts, airbag light, dash warning lights, climate, infotainment, windows, locks, horn, wipers, and odor
- Startup and Idle: Cold start behavior, idle quality, warning lights, exhaust color, and unusual odors
- Engine Section - Gasoline and Hybrid: Oil, coolant, belts, battery, leaks, radiator hoses, and fluid levels
- Engine Section - Diesel: Oil, coolant, DPF history, EGR, turbo, fuel filter, glow plugs, injectors, and DEF
- Hybrid System: High-voltage warning lights, battery history, regenerative braking, EV mode behavior, and PHEV charging
- Electric Vehicle System: High-voltage warnings, estimated range, charging port, DC fast charge, onboard charger, charging equipment, and battery history
- Test Drive: Acceleration, transmission shifts, steering, braking, noise, vibration, cruise control, and HVAC
- Post-Drive Review: Engine bay revisit, exhaust pipe check, fresh fluid drips, brake odor, and overall driving impression

POWERTRAIN-SPECIFIC SECTIONS

When you start an inspection, you select the powertrain type: Gasoline, Diesel, Hybrid, or Electric. The checklist automatically includes only the sections relevant to that powertrain. Diesel buyers get diesel-specific checks. Electric vehicle buyers get EV-specific checks. Hybrid buyers get both the gasoline engine section and the hybrid-specific section. No irrelevant sections clutter your workflow.

FIVE ITEM STATUS OPTIONS

For each checklist item, mark it as:
- Good: No issues observed
- Concern: Something to watch or investigate
- Critical Attention: A significant issue that needs resolution
- Not Checked: Could not check during this inspection
- Not Applicable: Not relevant to this vehicle

Add written notes to any item to record exactly what you observed.

DECISION STATUS TRACKING

Track where you stand on each vehicle with six decision options: Still Reviewing, Ask a Mechanic, Request More Info, Considering, Pass, or Purchased. See your decision at a glance from the saved inspections list.

SAVE MULTIPLE INSPECTIONS

Save and store as many inspection records as you need. Compare vehicles from different days or locations. Your records stay on your device - no account, no cloud, no expiration.

COMPARE UP TO 3 VEHICLES SIDE BY SIDE

Select up to three saved inspections and view a side-by-side comparison. See item counts by status, decision status, asking price, odometer, and key vehicle details at a glance. Export the comparison as a PDF to share with a spouse, family member, or advisor.

PDF INSPECTION REPORTS

Export a full PDF report for any inspection, including all vehicle details, every checklist item and its status, your written notes, and your decision status. Share the report via email, messaging, or any Android share target. PDFs are generated entirely on your device with no upload required.

JSON BACKUP EXPORT

Export any inspection record as a JSON file for personal backup or archiving.

WORKS COMPLETELY OFFLINE

All core features work with no internet connection. Create inspections, review checklists, save records, compare vehicles, and export PDF reports entirely offline. The only feature that requires internet is the display of ads.

FREE WITH ADS

Used Car Check Pro is free to download and use. It is supported by Google AdMob banner and interstitial ads. Ads appear on the home screen, saved inspections list, and vehicle comparison selection screen. Ads do not appear during active inspections, on checklist screens, on summary screens, or during data export. Interstitial ads are limited to once per session and once per 24 hours.

NO ACCOUNT REQUIRED

No sign-in, no registration, no email address, no cloud sync, and no subscription. Open the app and start inspecting.

PRIVACY

All inspection data is stored locally on your device. No personal information is collected or transmitted by the developer. The app uses Google AdMob for advertising, which operates according to Google's privacy policies.

DISCLAIMER

Used Car Check Pro is an observation and organization tool designed to help you record your findings during a used vehicle inspection. It does not replace a professional pre-purchase mechanical inspection. It does not replace a vehicle history report, recall check, or title search. It does not certify any vehicle as safe or roadworthy. It does not recommend whether to purchase any specific vehicle. Always consult a qualified mechanic and review all relevant records before making a purchase decision.
